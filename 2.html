Architectural Mitigations at 3nm

Details

1. Why Change is Needed

Because a 13nm architecture would fail almost immediately if simply shrunk to 3nm dimensions, foundries must implement major structural changes to guarantee a typical 5-to-10-year commercial lifespan.

2. The New Design (GAA)

Research indicates that the transition to Gate-All-Around (GAA) Nanosheet transistors at 3nm (moving away from the FinFETs used in 13nm/14nm) is heavily driven by these exact reliability requirements. GAA surrounds the channel on all four sides, regaining electrostatic control and mitigating short-channel effects.

3. Smart Power Control

Modern 3nm processors rely on aggressive workload-aware dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS) and thermal throttling. The internal systems adjust the chip's envelope in real-time, actively preventing the electrical/thermal conditions that accelerate TDDB and electromigration.
